# Howl

A social app for NC State students, built in the **Build Howl with AI** workshop for designers.

## Start here

Open the lesson and follow it step by step: **[lesson/index.html](lesson/index.html)**.

Everything after opening Copilot is copy and paste. You'll:

1. Find your taste and explore directions with AI
2. Build a design system with light and dark mode, and bring it into Figma
3. Lay out the app and remove the AI slop
4. Build the features: feed, posting, howls and campus spot filters
5. Go live on Vercel with a real Supabase database, plus a hidden preview link with sample data
